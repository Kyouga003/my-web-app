Frequently asked questions

How do I buy something?

Find a product you like, add it to your cart, and check out securely. You will receive an email confirmation with the order details.

How long does processing take?

Processing times vary by maker and are shown on each product listing. Most orders are shipped within a few business days of payment confirmation.

What if I need to cancel my order?

You can cancel most orders while they are still pending payment or paid. Contact the seller through your order details, and we will process any refunds.

How do I leave a review?

Once an order is completed, you will find a review link in your order details. Reviews are only allowed for purchases you actually made.

How do I sell my own work?

Create a free seller account, open a shop, and start listing products. Sellers manage inventory, fulfilment, and communication from their dashboard.

What payment methods do you accept?

Checkout is a sandbox flow for testing. Live payment providers can be configured by the marketplace administrator — see our support page.

How do I contact a seller?

Each product has a "Contact the maker" link on its detail page. Sellers are notified of your questions and can reply there.
