About Handmade & Co.

Handmade & Co. is a marketplace built for independent artists and makers. We believe the best things are made by hand — by people who care about their craft and the people who buy it.

Our marketplace exists to help makers reach buyers directly: no middlemen, no drop-shipping, no guesswork. Every product you see here is made by a real person who chose this shop to share their work with the world.

Whether you are here to find a one-of-a-kind gift or to start your own creative business, we are glad you are here.

Our promise

We review new listings, protect buyers, and keep the platform safe so you can shop with confidence.

Our standards

We expect every maker to sell honestly, respond to questions, and treat customers with respect.
