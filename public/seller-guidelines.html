Seller guidelines

These guidelines apply to every seller on Handmade & Co. They are the foundation of a fair marketplace.

Listing rules

  • Describe materials, dimensions, processing time, and any personalization clearly.
  • Picture your products well — no misleading or deceptive images.
  • Set a reasonable price for the quality and uniqueness of your work.

Order fulfilment

  • Mark orders as shipped only when they have left your hands.
  • Keep your stock accurate — overselling can be unsettling for buyers.
  • Respond to buyer questions promptly.

Community standards

  • No counterfeit, unsafe, or misrepresented items.
  • No contact with buyers outside the marketplace.
  • Respect each other and our shared community space.
